About SecureBank

SecureBank is a Banking Management System website prototype developed using HTML5 and CSS3. It demonstrates how banking information can be organized into clear, interconnected sections.

Our Aim

Provide a simple and user-friendly banking interface.

Our Vision

Make banking-related information easy to explore.

Our Technology

HTML5 for page structure and CSS3 for styling.

This is a student project, not a real banking service.
